Flavors & Stories of Al Karama
Welcome Home to

Authentic South Indian Flavours!

Established in 2023, Little Idly was born from a passion for bringing genuine South Indian vegetarian culinary heritage to Dubai. Nestled in the bustling heart of Al Karama, we serve slow-steamed idlys, crispy ghee roast dosas, and fragrant filter coffee crafted with home-style devotion.

Every grain is naturally fermented for 14 hours, every drop of ghee is 100% pure desi cow ghee, and every batch of sambar is simmered with freshly ground heirloom spices. No artificial shortcuts, no compromises — just timeless culinary warmth.
